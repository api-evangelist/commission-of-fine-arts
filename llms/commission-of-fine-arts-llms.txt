# U.S. Commission of Fine Arts

> Independent federal agency established in 1910 that advises the President, Congress, and the federal and District of Columbia governments on the design and aesthetics of construction in Washington, D.C., and on coins, medals and federal commemorative works. The CFA also appoints the Old Georgetown Board, which reviews design in the Georgetown Historic District. As of September 2026 the CFA publishes NO developer API, NO OpenAPI specification and NO developer program. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the agency's public web surface; it is not published by the CFA.

## Records and project review

- [Project Search](https://www.cfa.gov/records-research/project-search): server-rendered Drupal Search API view over roughly 19,500 project records. Filterable by full text, title and project type via GET query parameters (`search_api_fulltext`, `title`, `field_project_type`, `page`). There is no JSON, REST export or JSON:API endpoint behind it — the results are HTML only.
- [Records & Research](https://www.cfa.gov/records-research): the agency's records program.
- [Accessing CFA Records](https://www.cfa.gov/records-research/accessing-our-records): how to request records.
